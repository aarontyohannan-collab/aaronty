AARON TY — RESUME REPOSITORY DIRECTORY
======================================================================

To upload your resume to the portfolio:
1. Save your completed resume PDF file as:
   public/resume/resume.pdf

2. Once `public/resume/resume.pdf` exists:
   The website download buttons will automatically offer direct download
   of this file, and visitors will be able to download your verified resume.

3. Until then, the portfolio displays an elegant "Resume Coming Soon" 
   modal with your contact details (aaronty@gmail.com) and engineering focus.
